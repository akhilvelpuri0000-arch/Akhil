Privacy Policy — Ramapuram Saga

Last updated: 2026

Ramapuram Saga ("the App") is a word puzzle game. This page explains what information the App does and does not collect.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. All game data — including your chosen name, avatar, level progress, coins, streaks, and settings — is stored locally on your own device using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Backup Codes

The App offers an optional "Backup Code" feature that lets you export your progress as a text code so you can restore it after reinstalling the App. This code is generated and stored entirely on your device. It is your responsibility to save this code; we do not receive or store a copy of it.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the developer through the app listing page.
